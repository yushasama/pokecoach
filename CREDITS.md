## Methods and ideas we built on

- Transformer: Vaswani et al., "Attention Is All You Need", 2017.
- PPO: Schulman et al., "Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms", 2017.
- GAE: Schulman et al., "High-Dimensional Continuous Control Using Generalized Advantage Estimation", 2016.
- PFSP league and UPGO: Vinyals et al., "Grandmaster level in StarCraft II using multi-agent reinforcement learning" (AlphaStar), Nature 2019.
- V-trace (considered, not used): Espeholt et al., "IMPALA", 2018.
- The idea to fine-tune a behaviour-cloned policy with league RL against frozen opponents, and the observation that lethal search helps at play time, come from the 7th-place write-up (repository above) and the 8th-place solution write-up in the Kaggle competition discussion.
